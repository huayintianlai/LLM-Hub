# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want LLM-Hub to be a practical, welcoming open-source project for people building local AI tooling. Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and deployment constraints.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and bugs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, logs, or personal information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Bad-faith reports, spam, or abusive issue/PR behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block users when behavior harms the project or community.
